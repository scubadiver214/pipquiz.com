## Outcome

Home, account, hub, focus, and math are usable at 320, 768, and 1280 pixels, with the keyboard, and with reduced motion.

## Write paths

Existing components those screens use. No new routes. No API changes.

## Checklist

- 320px: no horizontal scroll on the five screens. Header becomes the menu button from slice 02.
- Touch targets on play screens stay at least 64px.
- Every control has a name. Icon-only buttons have `aria-label`.
- Focus rings are visible on the cream background.
- A `choose-one` item can be completed with Tab and Enter.
- Speak reads the prompt after the button press and does not speak on load.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables mascot bounce and confetti if any was added.
- Color is not the only cue on memory and shape items.
- The footer treatment line is still present.
